How the money reaches the wholesaler

Already on Paytm

Many wholesalers take payments on a Paytm QR. Paytm matches the GSTIN or phone number on the bill to that merchant and pays him there.

Bank details on the bill

Many bills print an account number and IFSC, or a UPI QR. The app reads them and pays by UPI.

Neither

The first time, the owner scans the salesman's QR. After that, every bill from that GSTIN goes to the same account.

Before any payment, the name the bank has for the account is checked against the name on the bill. Wholesalers usually give about 7 days of credit, so the owner can pay on the due date and Paytm reminds him the day before.

Stock credit, against stock Paytm can now see

Stock turnover

This shop sells its stock in about 5 days. A loan for stock is backed by stock that moves.

Payment record

Fourteen wholesaler bills paid through Paytm, on time.

Real bills

Bills from wholesalers above ₹5 crore turnover carry a GST e-invoice QR signed by the government. Paytm checks it, so a made-up bill can't raise credit.

Example: Paytm pays the wholesaler ₹15,000 today and the shop repays ₹508 a day from its QR for 30 days, 9% of its daily QR. The terms are only an example; a lending partner sets the real ones. More than half of Paytm's merchant loan disbursals already go to repeat borrowers (Q4 FY26 release).

Later: wholesalers send bills, and a better price for the same item

Wholesalers send the bill to Paytm

Most wholesalers bill from Tally, Marg or BUSY. A small connector on their side sends each bill to the shop's Paytm, with the items. Tally already lets other software read its data; BUSY and Marg have no public API yet.

Same item, cheaper

Munafa knows what each shop pays for each item. With IndiaMART, it can show sellers nearby quoting less for the same pack, and what that saves a month.

Tech stack

Capture

Camera in the Paytm for Business app; edge detection and de-skew on the phone

Read the bill

A vision language model returns item, qty, rate, MRP, GSTIN and bank details as JSON. Claude for the demo; an open model such as Qwen2.5-VL on Paytm's own servers in production

Find the payee

GSTIN and phone matched to Paytm merchants; UPI name check before every first payment

Check the bill

GST e-invoice QR signature verified, totals re-added, duplicate bills caught

Ledger

Bills, dues and payments per wholesaler; export to Tally XML, Excel and PDF

Munafa engine

Days between bills give the sales rate; margin × rate gives rupees a week; stock is bought minus sold

Voice

The model writes the note from the numbers; Hindi text-to-speech from an Indian voice model (Sarvam or Bhashini)

Pay and lend

UPI, with one-time mandates for due-date payments; stock credit through Paytm's lending partners
